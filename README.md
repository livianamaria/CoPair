# CoPair 

**Your next friend is one swipe away.**

CoPair is an Android app that helps gamers find friends, meet teammates, and connect through games and PC setups.

## Main Features

- **Discover:** swipe through player profiles based on shared games, playstyles, preferred roles, languages, and country. A mutual match opens a conversation.
- **Game Profiles:** add preferences for games such as LoL, CS2, Valorant, PUBG, GTA Online, Phasmophobia, and R.E.P.O.
- **Privacy Controls:** choose who you want to discover and who can discover you, with blocking and reporting options.
- **Voice Lobbies:** create temporary voice rooms for gaming, studying, or casual conversations. Set age and gender preferences, participant limits, and entry approval.
- **Community:** discuss games, PC builds, and peripherals through posts, questions, image polls, and comments.
- **Chats:** keep in touch with matches and gaming friends.

## Account & Profile

Create an account, choose a nickname, and complete your profile with an avatar, date of birth, country, gender, spoken languages, and favourite games.

## Device Features

- **Camera:** take a profile picture directly in the app.
- **Accelerometer:** shake the phone to undo the last rejected swipe.

## Technology

- Kotlin & Jetpack Compose
- Firebase Authentication, Firestore & Storage
- Separate files for screens, reusable UI components, and navigation

 In development. Features listed above describe the planned application.
